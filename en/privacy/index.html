Privacy policy

Updated on November 2, 2026

This is a personal website and it collects the minimum necessary. Here I explain, plainly, which data is processed, why, and how you control it, in line with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018).

1. Who is responsible

The party responsible for processing the data is Rodolfo Vasco, owner of the website rodolfovasco.com.br. For any privacy matter, write to rodolfovasco@gmail.com.

2. What data is processed and why

  • Browsing data, only with your consent. If you accept cookies, Google Tag Manager may activate measurement tools, such as Google Analytics. They record information such as pages visited, clicks on contact buttons, device and browser type, approximate location and IP address (processed by the provider). I use this to understand how the site is used and to improve it.
  • Data you send me. The site has no form. If you contact me through WhatsApp, email or LinkedIn, I process your name, your contact details and the content of your message only to reply and carry on the conversation.
  • Technical access data. The servers that deliver the site (hosted on GitHub Pages) and the font service (Google Fonts) receive your IP address and browser data so that the page can load. This is inherent to how the internet works.

If, in the future, I use advertising or remarketing tools, they will also only run with your consent, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

3. Cookies and your choice

On your first visit, the site shows a cookie notice. Without your consent, measurement and advertising tools stay off (Google Consent Mode v2).

  • Essential: rv_consent, stored in your browser's local storage, records whether you accepted or declined. It stays until you clear the site data. The same applies to rv_lang, which stores the language you chose.
  • Measurement (only with consent): Google Analytics cookies, such as _ga, when that tool is active in Tag Manager. Google sets the duration, usually up to 2 years.

You can change your mind at any time, or clear the site data in your browser.

4. Who the data may be shared with

With Google (Tag Manager, Analytics and Fonts), with GitHub (site hosting) and with the messaging platforms you choose to contact me. I do not sell personal data. Some of these providers are located outside Brazil, such as in the United States, and the transfer follows the LGPD, with the safeguards they offer.

5. How long the data is kept

Measurement data is kept for the period set in the tool itself and can be deleted on your request when you can be identified. Contact messages are kept for as long as needed for the conversation and to meet legal obligations.

6. Your rights

Under the LGPD, you can request:

  • confirmation that your data is being processed and access to it;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed in violation of the law;
  • data portability;
  • information about who the data has been shared with;
  • withdrawal of consent, at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, write to rodolfovasco@gmail.com. I reply within 15 days. You may also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) at gov.br/anpd. If you are located outside Brazil, you can write to the same address and I will handle your request in line with the applicable law.

7. Updates

I may update this policy when the site changes. The date of the latest revision is always at the top of the page.
